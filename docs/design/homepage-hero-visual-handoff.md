# Homepage Hero Visual Handoff

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Context

DataSolace is being repositioned from home automation / generic tech services toward small business process automation.

The public site should speak to UK small business owners, operators, and founder-led teams dealing with repeated admin, scattered tools, fragile handoffs, unclear ownership, and process work that relies too much on memory.

The brand should feel clear, assured, capable, boutique, personal, owner-led, and practical. It should be technically competent without feeling corporate or distant.

Accessibility matters. The site should remain readable for older users, use clear contrast, preserve predictable navigation, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

The preferred tone is boutique and personal, mixed with enough practical clarity that a new visitor immediately understands the offer.

The preview has now been simplified to one hero direction. The old comparison tabs were removed. The previous right-side workflow card board was also removed because it was too text-heavy and looked generic.

## Current Problem

The copy and layout are closer, but the hero visual is still weak.

The current right-side visual is an abstract process-line diagram showing messy inputs turning into cleaner outputs, with labels such as Email, Forms, Chasing, Intake, Follow-up, and Records.

It still feels generic and under-designed. The next pass should focus on stronger image generation, art direction, and visual metaphor.

## Goal For The Visual

The hero visual needs to communicate:

```text
DataSolace helps a small business turn messy everyday operations into a calmer, clearer way of working.
```

The visual should feel specific, polished, calm, and personal. It should support the boutique owner-led positioning, not read as generic SaaS automation.

## Avoid

- Generic SaaS workflow cards
- Zapier-style / no-code connector diagrams
- AI automation hype
- Corporate IT consultancy stock imagery
- Flat bookkeeping or admin software visuals
- Abstract diagram filler
- Walls of text inside the visual
- Overly glossy app-logo connector scenes

## Directions Worth Exploring

- A refined "owner's control room" scene: calm, practical, run by people who understand the work.
- A real-world small business operations desk: requests, notes, schedules, customer details, and documents becoming organized.
- A crafted before/after process map that feels editorial and tactile, not card-based.
- A visual showing inboxes, forms, schedules, files, and handoffs being clarified into one maintained process.
- A boutique consulting feel: direct, human, calm, capable.
- Subtle use of the DataSolace logo as a point of clarity, without making it a lazy centerpiece.

## Design Challenge

Find a hero visual metaphor that feels specific, polished, and personal without becoming another abstract automation diagram.

The image should make the business feel small enough to speak directly with the owners, but capable enough to trust with important operational systems.
